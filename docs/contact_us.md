# Contact us

Never hesitate to contact us!

These channels are available:

- [Slack](#slack)
- [GitHub](#github)
- [Email list](#email-list)
- [Email](#email)

## Slack

We have a public channel in the SciLifeLab Slack server
called `peer-observation-of-teaching`.

If you are not part of this server, [use email](#email) to request access.

## GitHub

You can [create an issue](https://github.com/SciLifeLab/peer_observation_of_teaching_group/issues)
or [contribute to this website](contributing.md)

## Email list

You can send your question to it: simply send an email to
`peer_observation_of_teaching@freelists.org`.

## Email
